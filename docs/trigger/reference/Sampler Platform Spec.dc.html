dandrumInstrument platform · Trigger spec

A Dandrum instrument is a curated composition of reusable engine primitives behind a purpose-built panel. Trigger, the advanced sampler, is the first instrument built this way. This document defines the model, the components, the interactions and the engine/UI contract. Screens live in Trigger mockup (23 states; toggle “Engine contract annotations” to see the mapping on every control).

0 · Assumptions and decisions

  • The Dandrum Design System is authoritative. All colours, type, radii, spacing, knobs, pads, KeyMap, LayerStack, OutputBusses and WaveformPanel come from it unchanged; additions are listed in section 3.
  • The demo patch “Felt Kit” deliberately combines all three use cases in one instrument: Keys (chromatic, 5 roots × 2 velocity layers), Drums (per-note sounds, velocity layer containing a round-robin group, hats in choke group 1) and Break (8 slices on C1–G1). One KeyMap shows all three because they are the same Trigger Rules.
  • The existing system’s “prepared vs live” split is kept: prepared structure (regions, mapping, selectors, voice policy) is edited here and committed on mouse-up; the engine re-prepares asynchronously and the status shows “Preparing…”. Live parameters change in real time and are host-automatable.
  • Mod slots A–D (colour + glyph) stay limited to four. They are assigned to the first four non-default modulators in a patch. Further modulators use a neutral hollow square and their name; every route row still carries text, so nothing relies on colour.
  • The terms Selector, Trigger Rule, Voice Template, Binding never appear in the default UI. They appear in “Edit template…”, in the annotation overlay and in this document.
  • No new engine APIs are defined. Section 6 classifies capabilities by concept only.
