    1|       |//! Version 1 editor admission and envelope encoding, used only off audio.
    2|       |//!
    3|       |//! Requests are JSON `{version:1, attachmentId:"1", requestId:"1", method, args}`.
    4|       |//! Identities are canonical decimal strings carrying full `u64` precision. The
    5|       |//! host supplies a distinct nonzero instance identity; native `SessionIdentity`
    6|       |//! and `Attachment` values remain the authority and are never serialized.
    7|       |//!
    8|       |//! Supported methods preserve the comparison frontend's positional arguments:
    9|       |//! - `getSamplerSession`, `getParameterState`, `noteHeartbeat`: `[]`.
   10|       |//! - `setInstrumentZoneKeyRange`: `[instanceId, attachmentId, sessionRequestId,
   11|       |//!   revision, mapId, zoneId, low, high]` (the first four are decimal strings).
   12|       |//! - `undoInstrumentSession`: `[instanceId, attachmentId, sessionRequestId, revision]`.
   13|       |//! - `getInstrumentSessionJobStatus`: `[sessionRequestId]`.

   19|       |//! native session's 16-attachment limit applies. Each attachment retains at most
   20|       |//! 128 typed transport requests. Accepted transport IDs increase independently
   21|       |//! of session job IDs. An identical retry returns `Duplicate` and must never be
   22|       |//! dispatched again; adapters recover structural outcomes through the existing
   23|       |//! session job identity. Retired transport IDs cannot be reused. Admission does
   24|       |//! not execute commands or admit structural work into `InstrumentSession`.
   25|       |//!
   26|       |//! Replies carry `{version, instanceId, attachmentId, requestId, status}` plus
   27|       |//! `result` or `error:{code}`. Events carry `{version, instanceId, attachmentId,
   28|       |//! sequence, event, payload}`. Event sequences are positive decimal strings,
   29|       |//! local to an attachment. Only `parameterStateChanged` and
   30|       |//! `instrumentSessionChanged` events are supported. Encoded output is at most
